# Code of Conduct

We want participation in nysos to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming,
regardless of background, experience, identity, or ability.

## Expectations

- Discuss ideas and behavior respectfully; give actionable feedback.
- Respect differing viewpoints and accept corrections in good faith.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing personal information.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or use sexualized language or imagery.
- Do not engage in personal attacks, unwanted contact, or sustained disruption.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project spaces, including public representation of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to **mccodeman@proton.me**, including relevant links or
context. Reports will be handled discreetly, with information shared only as
needed to investigate and respond. Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.

The maintainer may remove content, request changes, warn participants, or restrict
temporary or permanent participation based on severity and repeated behavior.
Participants may ask for reconsideration by contacting the same address with
additional context. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and explained
to the people involved when practical.
